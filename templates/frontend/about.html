ABOUT IOT HIVE

Making Smart Hardware & Electronics Accessible to Everyone

IoT Hive is an open community and marketplace where makers, hobbyists, students, and engineers come together to share, discover, and build smart gadgets and connected devices.

How IoT Hive Works

01

Find Projects & Guides

Browse through hundreds of working electronics projects with clear instructions, part lists, and circuit diagrams.

02

Build, Learn or Inquire

Use the shared guides to build your own gadgets, or message creators directly with questions or custom requests.

03

Share Your Own Builds

Post your own inventions to help other hobbyists. Set a price or share for free to build your portfolio and grow.

COMMUNITY FIRST

Designed for Everyday Makers

You do not need a degree in advanced robotics or complex terminology to build something amazing. We make it easy to discover blueprints, understand the parts required, and get help directly from creator communities.
