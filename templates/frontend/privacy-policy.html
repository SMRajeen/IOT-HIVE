Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to IoT HIVE. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored, and the choices available to you when using the IoT HIVE platform.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. About IoT HIVE

IoT HIVE is a community and marketplace platform for smart hardware, robotics, DIY electronics, IoT projects, connected devices, and related digital resources. Users may create accounts, publish projects, communicate with other users, submit inquiries, save projects, and interact with marketplace features.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use an IoT HIVE account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and username
  • Email address
  • Password and authentication-related information
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Profile and other images you upload

Information From Facebook Login

If you choose to sign in or register using Facebook Login, IoT HIVE may receive information that Facebook makes available to our application according to the permissions granted and your Facebook settings. This may include information such as your name, email address, and Facebook account identifier.

We use this information to authenticate you, create or connect your IoT HIVE account, maintain your account, and provide the requested services.

Content You Provide

We may collect information and content that you voluntarily submit to the platform, including project titles, descriptions, technical details, project images, documents, files, inquiries, messages, favorites, and other marketplace-related content.

Technical Information

We may receive technical information necessary to operate and secure the platform, such as browser information, device information, IP address, session information, request information, and server logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your IoT HIVE account
  • Authenticate users and provide login functionality
  • Provide Facebook Login when you choose to use it
  • Display and manage your profile
  • Publish and manage projects you submit
  • Process inquiries, orders, requests, and marketplace interactions
  • Allow users to save or favorite projects
  • Store and deliver uploaded project media and files
  • Send important account or service-related communications
  • Maintain platform security and prevent abuse
  • Diagnose errors and improve platform reliability
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Uploaded Files and Cloudinary

IoT HIVE uses Cloudinary as a third-party cloud service for persistent storage and delivery of user-uploaded media and files.

Depending on the feature being used, uploaded content may include profile images, project images, documents, ZIP files, CAD/STL files, firmware files, and other supported project resources.

Important: Uploaded files are stored using third-party cloud infrastructure so that they remain available when the application is deployed or restarted.

5. Database and Application Hosting

IoT HIVE uses cloud infrastructure to operate its application and store account and application data. PostgreSQL is used for structured application data, while the IoT HIVE application is hosted on Render.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Sessions

IoT HIVE may use cookies and session technologies necessary for authentication, security, maintaining login sessions, and providing core website functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

IoT HIVE may use third-party services to provide specific functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Meta/Facebook — authentication through Facebook Login
  • Cloudinary — storage and delivery of uploaded media and files
  • Render — application hosting
  • Neon/PostgreSQL infrastructure — application database services
  • Notification services — where required for platform communications

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared or made available when necessary to provide platform functionality, operate third-party services, protect the security of IoT HIVE, comply with applicable law, or respond to legitimate legal requests.

Information and content that you intentionally publish publicly on IoT HIVE, such as public project information and your public profile details, may be visible to other users or visitors.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction, deletion, or other changes to your information.

You should also review the information you choose to publish publicly before submitting it to IoT HIVE.

10. Data Deletion

If you want to request deletion of your IoT HIVE account and associated personal information, contact us using the contact method provided below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, to resolve disputes, or to maintain necessary transaction records.

11. Children's Privacy

IoT HIVE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information to IoT HIVE without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time when our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or a data deletion request, please contact the IoT HIVE team through the contact information provided by the platform.
